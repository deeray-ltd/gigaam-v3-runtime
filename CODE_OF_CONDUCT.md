# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to the people you work with here. Discuss technical work on its merits, assume
the other person is arguing in good faith, and remember there is a person on the other
side of every issue and review.

## Expected behaviour

- Focus criticism on code, designs and arguments — never on the person making them.
- Accept that people have different levels of context and experience; explain rather than
  dismiss.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, fix them, and move on without ceremony.
- Respect maintainers' time: a clear report, a reproducible case or a focused patch is
  worth more than urgency.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained hostility.
- Harassment of any kind, including unwelcome attention, and demeaning remarks about
  personal characteristics.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions or wasting contributors' time in bad faith.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and commit
messages — and to public spaces when someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **yury.krasilnikov@gmail.com**. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this document,
and may block a participant temporarily or permanently; a decision will be explained to
the person concerned.
